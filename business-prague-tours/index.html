A few practical questions

Who is this service for?

Companies welcoming international clients, business partners or colleagues to Prague. The tour is in English; arrangements can be discussed in Czech or English.

Can the tour fit around meetings?

Send your available time, starting point and any planned dinner location. We’ll shape the route together, with feasibility confirmed during planning.

Can you help with a restaurant?

I can recommend suitable options and help with a reservation by arrangement. A specific restaurant, table or time is subject to availability and confirmation. Restaurant bills are not automatically included in the guiding service.

How much does it cost, and what is included?

Price and scope are agreed around your brief. Before confirmation, we’ll clarify whether admissions, transport or any additional services are included. Corporate programmes are priced individually.
